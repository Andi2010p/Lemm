> ## ⚠️ THE ONE RULE
> **Never write "kids", "children", or "for young children" anywhere** — not here, not on the
> website, not in a screenshot caption, not in the app.

## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Lemma is an AI geometry tutor for secondary-school students, teachers, and anyone who wants to
learn geometry.

Scan, speak or type a geometry problem. Lemma explains it step by step — and DRAWS THE FIGURE in
2D and 3D, so you understand the problem instead of just copying an answer.


WHY LEMMA IS DIFFERENT

Ask a general AI chatbot a geometry question and you get paragraphs of text — and you still don't
know what the shape looks like.

Lemma draws it. A real diagram you can rotate, zoom and tap.


WHAT YOU GET

• It draws the figure — every solution comes with a real 2D or 3D diagram, not a wall of text
• Step by step — small, readable steps in plain language, the way a teacher explains it
• Scan from the textbook — point your camera at a printed problem and Lemma reads it
• Tap any theorem — theorem names in a solution are links to the proof, examples and a quiz
• Parametric sketcher — draw it yourself; change one length or angle and the shape adapts
• 3D workspace — pyramids, cones, cylinders, spheres and planes you can rotate and zoom
• Ask about any step — stuck on step 3? Tap it and ask. The tutor knows your problem and your figure
• Share with classmates — send a solved problem or a drawing to a friend or a study group
• Works on every device — your solutions, drawings and chats sync to your account


FOR TEACHERS

Run your class on Lemma. The Classroom plan seats up to 30 students, each with their own account
and their own progress. Invite them by username.


BUILT FOR THE CURRICULUM

A full theorem library for grades 7–12, with proofs, worked examples and quizzes.
Everything works in English, Russian and Armenian.


PLANS

Free — 3 solved problems every day, forever. The theorem library, the sketcher, your saved work,
and opening a solution a friend shared with you are always free.

Lemma Plus — 500 problems a month, no daily limit, and a smarter AI model.

Family — one payment, up to 6 people. Everyone keeps their own account and their own progress.

Classroom — one teacher, up to 30 students.


Lemma is intended for ages 13 and over.
Learn geometry properly. Don't just copy the answer.
```

## Content rating questionnaire

Answer honestly. With no ads, no violence, no gambling, and moderated user chat, Lemma should land
around **PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone** — but the **user-to-user messaging** means you must declare that
users can communicate, which typically nudges the rating and requires the reporting/blocking you
already built.
