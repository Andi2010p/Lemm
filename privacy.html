Lemma — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Lemma is a geometry learning app for students and teachers. It solves geometry problems, draws figures, and answers questions through an AI tutor. This policy explains exactly what data Lemma collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you delete it.

Lemma is operated by Narek Stepanyan ("we", "us"). Contact: lemmaofficial13@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

DataWhyWhen
Email address and username To create your account, sign you in, and send a one-time verification code Only if you register an account. You may use Lemma as a guest instead.
Geometry problems you type To generate the solution and drawing you asked for Each time you solve a problem
Photos you take or choose To read a printed problem or figure and solve it Only when you attach a photo. Lemma never accesses your gallery in the background.
Chat messages with the Lemma AI tutor To answer your questions and keep your chat history on the device Each time you send a message
Saved drawings and solutions To sync your work between your devices Only when you save an item
Purchase and AI-token balance To keep your subscription and token balance consistent across your devices When you purchase or use AI
Reports you submit about AI answers To detect and fix offensive or wrong AI output, as required by Google Play Only when you press "Report this answer"
Crash logs To fix crashes Stored on your device only

Lemma does not collect your location, contacts, microphone audio (speech is converted to text by your device's own keyboard/voice input), advertising ID, or browsing activity. Lemma contains no advertising and we do not sell your data.

2. Who we share it with

To provide the service, the content you submit is processed by these third parties, each under their own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database) — stores your account, saved work, token balance, and AI reports. Firebase privacy
  • Google Gemini API — receives the problem text and any photo you attach, in order to produce the solution. Gemini API terms
  • Google ML Kit — reads text from your photo. This runs on your device; the image is not uploaded for this step.
Optional: your own AI provider. In Settings you may choose to use OpenAI (GPT) or Anthropic (Claude) with your own API key. If you do, your problems and chat messages are sent to that provider instead of Google, under their terms and billed to your account, not ours. This is entirely your choice and is off by default.

We may also disclose data if required by law.

3. Children's privacy

Lemma is designed for school students, including children. We take this seriously:

  • We collect the minimum needed to run the app, and never for advertising or profiling.
  • There are no ads, no ad networks, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child without the consent of a parent, guardian, or teacher. Lemma can be used fully as a guest, with no email and no account.
  • If you believe a child has given us personal information without consent, email us and we will delete it promptly.

4. How long we keep it

Account data and saved work are kept until you delete them or delete your account. AI reports are kept while we investigate them. Crash logs stay on your device and only the most recent few are kept.

5. Deleting your data

You are always in control:

  • In the app: open Profile → Delete Account. This permanently erases your account and your cloud data (drawings, solutions, balances). It cannot be undone.
  • By email: write to lemmaofficial13@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete it and confirm within 30 days.
  • You can also delete individual saved solutions from the History screen at any time.

6. Security

Traffic between the app and our services is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us in plain text. Any AI provider key you enter yourself is stored only on your device.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at the address above and we will honour any such request.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where required, notify you in the app.

9. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: lemmaofficial13@gmail.com
