1. Age requirement

Lemma is intended for people aged 13 and over — secondary-school students, teachers, and anyone else who wants to learn geometry. It is not directed at children under 13, we do not market it to them, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

  • If we discover that we hold personal data of someone under 13, we delete it promptly.
  • If you believe a person under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we will remove it.
  • Where a younger student uses Lemma, it should be with the involvement of a parent, guardian or teacher.
  • Lemma can be used entirely as a guest — no email, no account, no personal data at all.

We collect the least we can get away with. There are no ads, no ad networks, no advertising ID, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in Lemma. We do not sell your data, and we never will.

3. Our legal basis (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — your account, your saved work, the AI solver, the messenger, and your subscription. Without this data the app cannot do what you asked it to.
  • Legal obligation and legitimate interests — abuse reports, blocking, and moderation. We have a duty (and a strong interest) in keeping a service used by teenagers safe.
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, and knowing what the service costs to operate.
  • Consent — optional things you choose to add, such as your school, your grade, or your own AI provider key. You can withdraw these at any time by clearing them.

5. Messages, chats and moderation — please read this

Your messages to other users are NOT end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by Google, and our security rules mean no other user can read your conversations. But we, as the operator, are technically able to access message content — and we will do so when we have to investigate an abuse report.

We are being blunt about this because you deserve to know, and because Google Play requires an app with user-to-user messaging to be able to act on reports of harmful content. An app used by teenagers that could not investigate bullying would not be a safer app — it would be a more dangerous one.

In practice:

  • We do not read your conversations for fun, for advertising, or to train AI.
  • We access message content when someone reports it, or where we must act on a credible safety or legal concern.
  • When you report a message, a copy of that message is sent to us as evidence, along with who sent it and who reported it.
  • You can block any user. A block is enforced by our servers, not just hidden in the app: a blocked person cannot message you or send you a friend request.
  • Messages cannot be edited or deleted once sent — this is deliberate, so that abuse cannot be erased before it is reported.

Your AI tutor chats are different: they are private to your account and no other user can read them.

6. The AI, and what happens to what you type

  • When you ask Lemma to solve a problem, the text of the problem — and any photo you attach — is sent to the AI provider to generate the answer.
  • Do not put personal or sensitive information into a problem or a chat. Type geometry, not secrets.
  • AI can be wrong. Lemma is a learning aid, not an authority. Always check its work — do not rely on it for an exam or a graded assignment without understanding it yourself.
  • If the AI produces something offensive or wrong, use the Report button. We review those reports and use them to improve safety filtering.

8. Deleting your data

You are always in control:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently erases your account and your cloud data — drawings, solutions, chats, balances. It cannot be undone.
  • Individual items: delete any saved solution or drawing from the History screen at any time.
  • Your school / grade: clear them in Profile → Edit details and you stop appearing in classmate suggestions.
  • By email: write to lemmaofficial13@gmail.com from the address on the account, subject "Delete my data". We will delete it and confirm within 30 days.

Note: deleting your account does not remove messages you already sent to other people from their copy of the conversation, and it does not erase abuse reports filed about you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct anything that is wrong
  • Erasure — have your data deleted (see §8)
  • Portability — receive your data in a usable format
  • Restriction and objection — limit or object to how we use it
  • Withdraw consent — for anything you gave us optionally
  • Complain — to your local data protection authority

Email lemmaofficial13@gmail.com and we will honour any such request, free of charge, within 30 days.

10. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). The app refuses plain-text connections entirely.
  • Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes (120,000 iterations). We cannot read your password, and neither can anyone who steals the database.
  • Database security rules enforce that you can only read your own data. These rules are covered by an automated test suite that we run before every change.
  • Verification codes are stored only as hashes, expire in 10 minutes, and are rate-limited.
  • Any AI provider key you enter yourself is kept on your device.
  • We use Google Play Integrity (App Check) to reject requests that do not come from a genuine, unmodified copy of Lemma.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please email us rather than exploit it — we will thank you and fix it.

11. Where your data is processed

Our database is hosted in the European Union (Firebase, europe-west1). Google may process some data (for example the AI request, and payments) on servers in the United States and elsewhere. Google relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards for these transfers.
