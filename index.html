How it works

From problem to understanding

Give Lemma the problem

Type it, speak it, attach a photo of the figure, or scan the page with your camera.

Watch it get drawn

Lemma works out the configuration, renders the figure, and lays the solution out in numbered steps with the final answer highlighted.

Ask until it clicks

Tap a step — or a part of the drawing — and ask about it. Tap a theorem to read its proof and take the quiz.

Keep it

Save it, export a PDF, or share it with your class. Everything syncs to your account.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What is Lemma?

Lemma is an Android app for secondary-school students, teachers, and anyone who wants to learn geometry. You scan, speak or type a problem, and Lemma explains it step by step and draws the figure — so you can see what the problem actually looks like instead of only reading about it. Lemma is intended for ages 13 and over.

How is Lemma different from ChatGPT or Gemini?

A general chatbot answers a geometry question with text. Lemma draws the actual figure — a real 2D or 3D diagram you can rotate, zoom and tap — and links every theorem it uses to a page with the proof and a quiz. It's built around a school geometry curriculum for grades 7–12, not for general conversation.

Is Lemma free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 3 solved problems every day. The theorem library, the sketcher, your saved work, and opening a solution a friend shared with you are always free. Paid plans add more problems and a smarter AI model.

Does Lemma work in Armenian?

Yes — fully. The interface, the step-by-step explanations and the theorem library all work in Armenian, Russian and English.

Can it solve a problem from a photo of my textbook?

Yes. Point the camera at a printed problem. Lemma reads the text, rejects images that aren't geometry, then draws and solves it.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 7.0 or newer.
