Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lemma completely offline-capable?

Yes! You can sketch, dimension, edit, and review your saved history completely offline. When you log back in on a connected device, our Cloud Sync Manager automatically pushes your offline work to the database.

What is a Parametric Sketcher?

Unlike simple paint apps, Lemma stores lines as mathematical relations. When you select a line and change its length (e.g. from 50 to 100), connected lines are automatically stretched and dragged with it, maintaining your geometry.

How can I run the AI for free?

To keep the app free, you can go to Settings and enter your own personal Gemini API Key, or unlock Lemma Pro by using the secret 5-tap developer shortcut!
